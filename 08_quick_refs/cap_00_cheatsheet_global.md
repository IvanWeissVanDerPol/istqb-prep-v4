## GLOSARIO MNEMOTÉCNICO

- **7 Principios:** "S-E-E-C-P-C-A" (Show presence not absence, Exhaustive impossible, Early saves time/money, Cluster, Pesticide, Context-dependent, Absence-of-errors is fallacy)
- **Niveles:** "CISA" (Component, Integration, System, Acceptance)
- **Inspection 5 roles:** "MARS" (Moderator, Author, Reviewer, Scribe)
- **Inspection 6 phases:** "P-K-I-R-F"
- **Risk responses:** "AMTA" (Accept, Mitigate, Transfer, Avoid)
- **Risk level:** "L × I" (Likelihood × Impact)
