## 📄 Step 1: Build your portfolio (no code needed)

**Portfolio = proof you can do the work.** For QA, this means: bug reports.

### The "Bug Report Portfolio" project (do this in week 5-6 of study)

Pick 3 real apps on your iPhone. Could be anything:
- PedidosYa / Uber Eats (delivery)
- Personal Pay / Tigo Money (banking)
- A chat app (Signal, Telegram, etc.)
- A Paraguayan government app (like the vaccination certificate app)

For each app, write **5 real bug reports** in this format:

```
────────────────────────────────────
🐛 BUG REPORT #1
App: PedidosYa iOS
Date: 2026-08-22
Tester: [your name]

TITLE: Coupon from 2022 still applies 50% discount

SEVERITY: Medium
PRIORITY: High

ENVIRONMENT:
- Device: iPhone 14, iOS 17.4
- Network: Wi-Fi
- App version: 5.2.1

STEPS TO REPRODUCE:
1. Open PedidosYa
2. Go to "Promotions"
3. Apply coupon code "VERANO2022"
4. Try to checkout

EXPECTED: App says "coupon expired"
ACTUAL: Applies 50% discount successfully

ATTACHMENT: [screenshot saved as bug_01.png]
────────────────────────────────────
```

**That's it.** Do this for 15 bugs (5 per app, 3 apps).

**Put them in a Google Doc or Notion page** → that's your portfolio. Share the link on your CV.

Why this works:
- Proves you can write bug reports clearly
- Shows you actually USE apps carefully (most candidates don't)
- Gives recruiters something to look at
- Takes 1 weekend

## 🤖 How AI changes your job hunt (and your job)

This is important. AI is transforming QA in **3 ways** you need to know about.

### 1. AI as your job-hunt assistant (use it TODAY)

You can use free AI tools (ChatGPT free, Claude, Gemini) to:

- **Improve your CV:** Paste your draft → ask "rewrite this for a junior QA role, ATS-friendly"
- **Write cover letters:** "Write a cover letter for [job title] at [company], based on my CV"
- **Practice interviews:** "Give me 5 ISTQB interview questions and rate my answers"
- **Translate your portfolio to English:** "Translate this bug report to English, keep technical terms"

**Real example:** You find a job posting. Paste the posting + your CV into AI. Ask: *"Write me a cover letter. Keep it 200 words. Mention I'm ISTQB certified. Mention my animal-care background gives me detail skills."* Done in 30 seconds.

### 2. AI as a QA tool you'll USE on the job

Modern QA testers use AI to:
- **Generate test cases from requirements:** "Here's a feature spec. Generate 10 test cases." AI does this in 5 seconds.
- **Find bugs faster:** AI tools (like Playwright AI, Mabl, Testim) can auto-detect UI anomalies.
- **Write bug reports:** You dictate what you saw → AI formats it into a structured bug report.

**This is GOOD news for you.** You don't need to "compete" with AI — you USE it. A QA tester who knows AI tools gets paid more than one who doesn't.

### 3. The new cert: CT-GenAI (after you pass CTFL)

ISTQB released a new certification in 2025: **CT-GenAI** (Testing with Generative AI).

It covers:
- How to use AI to generate tests
- How to validate AI outputs (do they hallucinate? are they biased?)
- Risks of AI in software

**Take this AFTER CTFL.** It's optional, but it puts you ahead of 95% of testers.

## 🎯 Skills to add in your first 12 months (in order)

After ISTQB, your next skills to learn, in order:

### Month 1-2: SQL basics
- Free: https://sqlbolt.com (2 hours)
- Free: Khan Academy SQL course
- **Why:** Every app has a database. QA testers query databases to verify data.

### Month 3-4: Test automation basics (Playwright)
- Free: https://playwright.dev (official docs)
- Free course: "Playwright with TypeScript" on YouTube
- **Why:** Doubles your salary. Lets you apply to mid-level roles.

### Month 5-6: API testing (Postman)
- Free: https://learning.postman.com
- **Why:** Most apps are APIs. QA testers who know API testing are in demand.

### Month 7-9: Build a portfolio of automated tests
- Push your code to GitHub
- Each project: a small app + your automated test suite
- This becomes your new CV

### Month 10-12: Apply to mid-level roles
- New target salary: USD 50,000-80,000/year remote
- Apply to the same platforms (LinkedIn, Toptal, Arc.dev, etc.)

## � Interview questions you'll be asked

(From `15_interview_prep/README.md` — same repo)

Top 10 questions:

1. **What are the 7 principles of testing?** → memorize
2. **Error vs Defect vs Failure?** → table
3. **Verification vs Validation?** → "building right" vs "building the right thing"
4. **What's regression testing?** → re-test after changes
5. **How would you test a login screen?** → walk through steps (your answer here is the test!)
6. **What is a decision table?** → table of conditions and actions
7. **Test cases for a phone number field?** → equivalence partitioning, edge cases
8. **Severity vs Priority?** → impact vs urgency
9. **What tools have you used?** → Jira, GitHub, browser DevTools
10. **Why do you want to be a QA tester?** → use your story (animal care → detail)

**Practice these out loud** in English. Use your iPhone voice memo to record yourself.
