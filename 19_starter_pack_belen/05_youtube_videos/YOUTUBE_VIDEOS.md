# 📺 Best YouTube videos to start your QA journey

*Hand-picked, beginner-friendly, English. Watch in this order on your iPad/iPhone.*

---

## 🎯 How to use this list

- All videos are **in English** (your friend speaks English ✓)
- All are **free on YouTube**
- They go from "what is QA?" → "what a day looks like" → "study for ISTQB" → "do the actual work"
- **Suggested schedule:** 1-2 videos per day during week 1 of study (instead of pure reading)

**Pro tip for iPad/iPhone:** Open YouTube in the app → tap "Add to Queue" → build a playlist called "QA Bootcamp" → watch 1-2 per evening with subtitles on.

## ⚠️ What NOT to watch yet

- **Anything 5+ years old** — covers old ISTQB v3.1, wrong terminology
- **"Become a QA tester in 30 days"** — usually misleading
- **Heavy automation tutorials** — overwhelming before you have the basics
- **Coding bootcamp channels** — they're for developers, not testers

## 📱 iPhone/iPad tips for watching

- **Subtitles ON** — non-native speakers benefit a lot
- **Speed = 1.25x** — most QA videos are slow-paced; 1.25x is the sweet spot
- **Picture-in-Picture** — watch while taking notes in Apple Notes
- **"Save to Watch Later"** — bookmark now, watch when commuting

## 💬 One last note

You don't need to watch ALL of these. The first 5 are essential. The rest are for when you need them.
