# 📅 Your 6-Week ISTQB Plan (made for you)

*You're between jobs right now — this plan uses ~25-30 hours/week. Realistic but moves fast.*

---

## 🎯 The goal

By the end of week 6, you're ready to take the **ISTQB CTFL v4.0.1** exam in Asunción.

- **40 questions**, multiple choice
- **60 minutes**
- **65% to pass** (26 correct)
- **Cost:** ~USD 200 (paid to ASOLINFO Paraguay)
- **Valid for life**
- **Available in Spanish** — but you can do it in English too

The official material is here (free PDF):
→ https://istqb.org/certifications/certified-tester-foundation-level/

Your friend already organized a complete study pack at:
→ https://github.com/IvanWeissVanDerPol/istqb-prep-v4

I'll tell you exactly which files to open, in what order, on which day.

---

## 📱 How to study on your iPhone/iPad

Before week 1:

1. **Install Anki** (free on App Store) — for flashcards
2. **Install the GitHub app** — to read the study files
3. **Install Forest** or a focus timer — to block distractions
4. **Bookmark** https://github.com/IvanWeissVanDerPol/istqb-prep-v4 in Safari

That's it. No PC needed. You can do everything from your iPad.

## 📅 Week 2 — "Cap 4 in depth" (the heaviest chapter)

**Time:** ~30 hours

Cap 4 = 30% of the exam. **Don't rush this week.**

### Daily routine (5-6 hours/day)

**Hour 1 — Read theory:**
- `05_summaries/cap_04_tecnicas_diseno_v4.md` (re-read sections, take notes)

**Hour 2 — One technique at a time:**
The chapter covers 4 main techniques. Spend a day on each:

| Day | Technique | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Equivalence Partitioning (EP)** | Find 3 examples in real apps. Write 5 test cases per example. |
| Tue | **Boundary Value Analysis (BVA)** | Same. 3 examples, 5 test cases each. |
| Wed | **Decision Tables** | Build a decision table for: "Can I return this product? (days, receipt, condition)" |
| Thu | **State Transitions** | Draw an ATM diagram: states + transitions. List valid + invalid paths. |
| Fri | **Statement/Branch Coverage** | Use the example in the cheatsheet — try without notes. |

**Hour 3-4 — Practice quiz:**
- `06_practice_tests/quizzes_por_capitulo/cap_04_quiz.md` — take it again, score it

**Hour 5 — Hard mode (optional but recommended):**
- `06_practice_tests/quizzes_por_capitulo/cap_4_5_hard_mode.md` — only after you've done the regular quiz

**Hour 6 — Review:**
- Whatever you got wrong today → go back to the summary → re-read that section

### Day 6-7 (Sat-Sun) — Anki + cheatsheet (5h)

- Open `04_flashcards/flashcards_v4.0.1.csv` on your computer → import into Anki (use the guide: `04_flashcards/anki_import_guide.md`)
- Review all flashcards for Cap 1, 2, 3, 4 (you should have ~30-40 now)
- Open `08_quick_refs/cap_04_cheatsheet.md` — print it or keep it in Notes

**End of Week 2 checkpoint:** ≥75% on Cap 4 quiz. If no → spend another 3 days on Cap 4 before Cap 5.

## 📅 Week 4 — "Sample exams + fix weak spots"

**Time:** ~25 hours

This is where you find out what you actually know.

### Day 1 — Sample Exam A (3h)

- When the timer ends, stop. Score yourself.
- Read the answer explanations: `06_practice_tests/sample_exam_A_ANSWERS.md`

- For each wrong answer → find the chapter → re-read that section
- Add those concepts to your Anki deck

### Day 3 — Sample Exam B (3h)

- Same as Day 1, with Sample Exam B
- Score. Fix.

### Day 4 — Sample Exam C (3h)

- Same, Sample Exam C
- Score. Fix.

### Day 5 — Re-do your weakest (5h)

**End of Week 4 checkpoint:** All 3 sample exams at ≥70%. If not → spend 2-3 more days on the weak chapter.

You're going to fail some quizzes. That's **by design**. The quizzes are there to show you what you don't know yet. A 50% on a quiz in week 1 is information, not failure.

The exam only needs 65%. You don't need to be perfect. You need to be **consistent and prepared.**
